Opportunity · Predoctoral Milling
Predoctoral students rarely mill, while the graduate program mills routinely. One route to close that gap is to give full credit for milling a composite block and luting with glass ionomer as a long-term provisional, which would put chairside milling in the hands of every predoctoral student. Increasing lithium disilicate inlays and onlays is a second route, and both use capacity that is already installed and paid for.

Evaluates student preparations against the program's actual course rubric and criteria. Planmeca Compare provides geometric overlays but does not grade to program-specific rubrics. Grade FX closes that gap.

Digital dentistry accounts for 67.8 of 2,294 topic hours across 10 of 25 courses. It is the smallest of the major restorative topic areas, well behind Operative Dentistry (297 h), Fixed Partial Dentures (249 h), and Dental Materials (248 h). Expanding digital contact hours is a curriculum priority.
Figures are curriculum committee topic allocations. A session-level count returns 59.3 hours across 31 sessions and 9 courses, the difference reflecting how multi-topic sessions are attributed.
New competency
A competency in digital scanning and intraoral photography was created for Dental Hygiene and is already implemented for the Class of 2027.
Extends digital adoption beyond the predoctoral program into hygiene. A concrete, implemented educational outcome tied to a specific cohort.
