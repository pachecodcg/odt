Adoption is reported across three bands. Student adoption is unchanged. Faculty adoption moved up slightly again this period and remains the primary constraint on the overall figure.

Why the faculty needle moved
More faculty are reporting scanner use directly to the office, mostly in the faculty practice. In the Simulation Lab, scanner use rose sharply while complaints fell, an inverse relationship that signals the workflow is settling in. Faculty are also expressing more interest in training and in the micro-credential.

Positions are informed estimates as of September 2026. No measured percentage is available. A measured figure, drawn from Romexis and Formlabs usage logs against available capacity, is planned for future reports. The faculty survey and the micro-credential program remain the active levers.

Capacity Risk
Predoctoral in-house crown production remains at zero. The milling technician line has now been open for 14 months, since August 2025. The CAD designer position (Brian's position) is also open. Without either role, the four milling units and the PlanCAD and exocad licenses sit idle for predoctoral cases, and milling requests from faculty and residents go unserved.
Fabrication demand
A steady 4 to 5 fabrication requests per week continue to arrive from faculty, residents, and research groups. Since Emma's return on August 25 the printing requests are being served. The milling requests are not, because there is no one to run the mills. Examples carried from August: the Diagnostic Sciences occlusal device workflow, the pediatric space maintainer pilot, Grad Pros denture resins, and the NIH R03 mouse palatal appliances.
