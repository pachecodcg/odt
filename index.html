Adoption is reported across three bands. Student adoption currently sits in the green band . Faculty adoption remains the primary constraint on the overall figure, but it moved up this period, edging out of the red band .

Why the faculty needle moved
After a single training session with one dental hygiene professor, she gathered the hygiene faculty in the clinic on her own and they scanned each other with no request and no dedicated training. In RSOC 5311 , intraoral scanning drew strong student feedback and greater faculty participation with no complaints, a reversal from last year. This is the model for how adoption should spread.

Following conversations with Interim Dean Gonzalez on the direction of digital technologies at DCG, the decision was made not to surplus the 3D printers . The shared priority is educational and research access to the printers and milling units, opening them to residents, faculty, and students as much as possible so they can innovate. The conversation on expanding access continues.

Opportunity · Predoctoral Milling
Predoctoral students rarely mill, while the graduate program mills routinely. One route to close that gap is to give full credit for milling a composite block and luting with glass ionomer as a long-term provisional, which would put chairside milling in the hands of every predoctoral student. Increasing lithium disilicate inlays and onlays is a second route, and both use capacity that is already installed and paid for.

Evaluates student preparations against the program's actual course rubric and criteria . Planmeca Compare provides geometric overlays but does not grade to program-specific rubrics. Grade FX closes that gap.
