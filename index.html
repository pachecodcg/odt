Adoption is reported across three bands. Student adoption is unchanged. Faculty adoption moved up slightly again this period and remains the primary constraint on the overall figure.

Why the faculty needle moved
More faculty are reporting scanner use directly to the office, mostly in the faculty practice. In the Simulation Lab, scanner use rose sharply while complaints fell, an inverse relationship that signals the workflow is settling in. Faculty are also expressing more interest in training and in the micro-credential.

Positions are informed estimates as of September 2026. No measured percentage is available. A measured figure, drawn from Romexis and Formlabs usage logs against available capacity, is planned for future reports. The faculty survey and the micro-credential program remain the active levers.

Capacity Risk
Predoctoral in-house crown production remains at zero. The CAD/CAM designer line has now been open for 15 months, since August 2025, and the milling technician role is unfilled. The Center plan (Ask 1) asks for the designer to be posted by November 14. Without either role, the four milling units and the PlanCAD and exocad licenses sit idle for predoctoral cases, and milling requests from faculty and residents go unserved.
Fabrication demand
A steady 4 to 5 fabrication requests per week continue to arrive from faculty, residents, and research groups. Since Emma's return on August 25 the printing requests are being served. The milling requests are not, because there is no one to run the mills. Examples carried from August: the Diagnostic Sciences occlusal device workflow, the pediatric space maintainer pilot, Grad Pros denture resins, and the NIH R03 mouse palatal appliances.

New Functional
What it does
A browser tool that checks whether bridge abutment preparations draw together along a chosen path of insertion. Load the arch STL, tag the abutments by tooth number, then drag the path arrow or tilt it along and across the bridge (up to 30°). Surfaces that would block seating show in red; walls within 2° of parallel are flagged separately. A Best path search finds the direction with the least undercut and reports whether the bridge is clear or blocked.
Readout
Angle from vertical along and across the bridgedegrees
Undercut area of the analyzed abutment surfacemm²
Draw statusClear or Blocked
ScopeSelected abutments or whole arch
Why it matters: students and residents can test a bridge preparation for a common path of insertion before the case leaves the sim lab or the Esthetic Lab, with the same STL used for grading. Built in house on Three.js, single HTML file, no install; light and dark modes in the AU palette. Demo for the November 4 Deans' meeting.
